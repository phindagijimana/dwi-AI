    Connectomics¶

    Connectomics is the project of mapping neural connections at a chosen scale (Sporns, Tononi & Kötter 20051; Lichtman & Sanes 2008 at microscale; Hagmann 2008 and the Human Connectome Project at macroscale23).

    This tool lives at macroscale, in vivo, diffusion MRI. The matrix \(W\) that nodestrength consumes is not “the connectome” in the synaptic sense. It is a reconstruction. This page is how that reconstruction is made, why two labs’ \(W\) disagree, and which literatures sit next to it.

    Scales of connectomics (do not mix them)¶

    Scale Object Methods Landmark
    Micro Synapses, neurites EM (White 1986 C. elegans; FlyEM; MICrONS) Lichtman & Sanes 2008
    Meso Projection neurons, tracers Viral tracers, CLARITY, CoCoMac, Allen CCFv3 Markov et al. macaque; Oh et al. mouse
    Macro Grey-matter parcels, white-matter pathways dMRI tractography, polarised light, dissection Hagmann 2008; HCP

    Jones, Knösche & Turner (2013)4 is the paper to read before treating a streamline count as an axon count.

    How a structural connectome is made¶

    flowchart TD
  RAW[Raw dMRI] --> PRE[denoise, unring, eddy, TOPUP]
  PRE --> FOD[FOD / MSMT-CSD]
  T1[T1w] --> PARC[Parcellation]
  T1 --> ACT[5TT ACT]
  FOD --> TCK[tckgen]
  ACT --> TCK
  TCK --> FILT[SIFT2 / COMMIT]
  PARC --> C[tck2connectome]
  FILT --> C
  C --> W["W, the connectome"]
    1. Preprocess the diffusion data (motion, eddy, susceptibility). QSIPrep (Cieslak et al. 20215) is the current standard BIDS implementation.
    2. Estimate fibre orientations. DTI (Basser, Mattiello & LeBihan 1994) gives one ellipsoid per voxel — insufficient at crossings. HARDI / CSD (Tournier, Calamante & Connelly 2007) and MSMT-CSD (Jeurissen et al. 20146) give fibre orientation distributions (FODs) that can support multiple peaks.
    3. Grow streamlines. Deterministic: follow the peak (FACT, SD_STREAM). Probabilistic: sample the FOD (iFOD2 in MRtrix3). Anatomically constrained tractography (ACT; Smith et al. 20127) uses a 5-tissue-type image so streamlines terminate in grey matter rather than running into CSF.
    4. Filter. Raw tractograms over-represent easy paths. SIFT (Smith 20138) removes streamlines; SIFT2 (Smith 20159) keeps them all and assigns a weight so the FOD is explained. COMMIT (Daducci 201510) and LiFE (Pestilli 2014) are convex / biological alternatives.
    5. Parcellate and count. tck2connectome sums (weighted) streamlines between pairs of grey-matter labels.

    MRtrix3 (Tournier et al. 201911) is the usual implementation of 2–5. QSIRecon wraps those recipes in BIDS.

    Deterministic vs probabilistic¶

    Deterministic Probabilistic (iFOD2)
    Algorithm Follow the FOD peak Sample the FOD
    Typical \(W\) Sparser; weaker long-range Denser; more long-range
    False negatives Kissing / crossing missed Fewer misses
    False positives Fewer hallucinations Long-range invented connections
    Strength / AI Different numbers on the same subject Different numbers on the same subject

    They are different experiments. A bitwise-identical Count matrix labelled both “det” and “prob” is a packaging error, not a replication. True deterministic Count on the same subjects is sparser and will move ROC AUC. IDEAS Figshare dumps have illustrated both the error (old “det” copied from prob) and the real difference (later true det zip).

    What tractography cannot promise¶

    Read these before writing “we mapped the connectome”:

    • Jones, Knösche & Turner 2013 — fibre count and other fallacies4
    • Thomas et al. 2014, PNAS — anatomical accuracy is inherently limited12
    • Maier-Hein et al. 2017 — international tractography challenge; even experts disagree13
    • Schilling et al. 2019 — modern methods still limited14
    • Yeh et al. 2021 — challenges and opportunities review15
    • Reveley / Jbabdi / Calabrese — gyral bias, bottleneck issues, ground-truth animal comparisons

    A zero in \(W\) means “this reconstruction did not draw a connection,” not “biology has no axons here.” A large \(w_{ij}\) means “many weighted streamlines ran between these parcels,” not “this is a strong synaptic projection.”

    Edge weights are different graphs¶

    On the same tractogram you can write several matrices. They are not interchangeable biomarkers.

    Weight How it is made What \(s_i=\sum_j w_{ij}\) then means
    Count Number of streamlines Raw reconstructed wiring mass; scales with tckgen count
    SIFT2-weighted count Streamline weights from SIFT2 Preferred quantitative structural edge (Smith 2015)
    CountScaled Count ÷ (volume and/or length) Attempts to correct parcel size; name the divisor
    Mean FA Average FA of voxels / streamlines on the path Mixes whether a path exists with microstructure of that path
    MD / RD / AD Same with other tensors Diffusivity load, not wiring mass
    Mean length Average streamline length Total millimetres of track, not connection weight

    Gong et al. (2009)16 and Hagmann (2008) discuss weighted vs binary cortical networks. Never concatenate Count AI and MeanFA AI in one ROC without labelling the edge. In IDEAS-style trees you will see Count, CountScaled, MeanFA, MD, Length as separate experiments.

    Parcellations used with this software¶

    Desikan–Killiany and fs_default¶

    Desikan et al. (2006)17: 34 cortical gyri per hemisphere from FreeSurfer aparc, plus subcortical aseg structures. MRtrix3 fs_default.txt is an 84-row lookup used by labelconvert. dkt-ai-cohort pairs homologous L/R labels on that grid. Thalamus is one node per side (Thalamus-Proper). Per-nucleus thalamus requires THOMAS (or similar) on top — that is the Piper atlas, not DK.

    Lausanne 2008 / Cammoun¶

    Hagmann 2008 and Cammoun et al. 201218: recursive subdivision of DK cortex. Published scales are approximately 36, 60, 125, 250 cortical parcels per hemisphere-style naming; after dropping brainstem and using both hemispheres plus subcortex, connectome sizes in easy_lausanne GraphML (this project) are 82 / 128 / 233 / 462 nodes. Hippocampus is not split. Row order is GraphML order: drop Brain-Stem, then left subcortical, right subcortical, left cortex, right cortex — not FreeSurferColorLUT.txt order. Matching node counts is not matching LUT.

    THOMAS thalamic nuclei¶

    Su et al., Thalamus Optimized Multi Atlas Segmentation19. Piper et al. 2026 merge THOMAS nuclei with cortical Lausanne/DK-style labels to ask which nucleus is under-connected20. Whole-thalamus DK AI cannot answer that.

    Other atlases you will meet¶

    AAL (Tzourio-Mazoyer 2002), Harvard-Oxford, Schaefer 201821, Glasser MMP 201622, Brainnetome (Fan 2016), AICHA, Yeo 7/17 (functional, not structural). Zalesky 2010: node choice changes every subsequent metric.

    Functional connectomics (not computed here)¶

    Resting-state edges are typically Pearson or partial correlations of ROI time series (Biswal 1995; Smith et al. 201123). Confounds: motion (Power et al. 201224), global signal, atlas, pipeline (Ciric / Parkes “denoising” papers). Graph metrics can be computed on that matrix; thresholding and density are a literature of their own (van Wijk 201025). NBS (Zalesky, Fornito & Bullmore 201026) is the standard edgewise inference tool — see Other methods.

    Do not run strengths_und on a Fisher-z matrix and interpret it as streamline strength.

    Epilepsy and the connectome¶

    Focal epilepsy is a network disease as much as a focal lesion (Spencer 2002; Richardson 2012; Bernhardt, Bonilha & Gross 201527; Tavakol et al. 2019). Relevant strands:

    • Hippocampal sclerosis: volume loss and T2; laterality of volume is the classical MRI biomarker. Connectivity AI asks whether remaining tissue is also under-wired (Asymmetry).
    • Thalamocortical loops: absence and focal seizures both implicate thalamus; Piper et al. 2026 measure thalamocortical structural strength in children with focal epilepsy, with SOZ and mixed-design GLM20.
    • Graph metrics in epilepsy: reviews report altered clustering, efficiency, and hubs — with all the density caveats of the primer. This software does not add those metrics by default.
    • IDEAS I/II: Lausanne-scale structural connectomes used in this repository to apply strength+AI to hippocampus (Count/FA/Length, det vs prob, scales 36–250).

    Practical checklist before you score \(W\)¶

    1. Named reconstruction (QSIPrep version, MRtrix3 version, iFOD2 vs FACT, SIFT2 on/off, streamline count).
    2. Named atlas, scale, LUT provenance.
    3. One weight type per contrast.
    4. Det and prob never pooled unlabeled.
    5. Diagonal zeroed, matrix square, symmetrised if undirected.
    6. Then — and only then — node strength.
